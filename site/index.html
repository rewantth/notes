      About this page

      This page fills itself in. About every 10 minutes a small program reads your job-related emails in Gmail (read-only), works out which site each application came from and what the latest news is, and refreshes the page.

      It reads patterns in the sender and the wording, so now and then it misreads an email. A ? after a company means the name was a guess. The Gmail link always opens the original.

      Your list is locked with your passphrase and unlocked here, in your browser. It never sits anywhere in plain text.

      Shortcuts: Ctrl + K or / opens the search box.
